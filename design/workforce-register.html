Legal & Compliance(a) 100% on-time filing and payment of statutory obligations (PF, ESI, PT, TDS, etc.). (b) Complete exit formalities within 7 working days of resignation/last working day. (c) Process accurate Full & Final settlements within 30 days of exit, with zero compliance lapses.
